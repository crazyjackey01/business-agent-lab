# Soundway customer-support procedures

Fictional internal procedure. Version 1.0, effective 1 September 2026.

## SUP-01 Wrong-item reports

1. Collect the order number, ordered model, delivery date, and a photo showing the received item and its model label.
2. Check the order record against the photo to verify the mismatch.
3. Ask whether the customer wants the ordered model or a refund. Do not assume the customer's choice.
4. For a replacement, check stock and confirm the delivery address. Apply the shipping guide.
5. Arrange the approved remedy and a prepaid return label through the authorized support system.

A verified wrong-item claim reported within 14 calendar days needs no manager approval. Claims reported later require manager review. Agents without access to the support system can recommend these steps but cannot claim to have completed them.

## SUP-02 Defect reports

Collect purchase details, the model, and a description of the fault. Check the product's warranty period and submit the report for warranty review. Do not choose a remedy before the review.

## SUP-03 Missing information

State what the documents establish and ask for facts needed for the next decision. Do not invent order details, stock levels, customer addresses, or company policies. Cite the document section used for each policy-dependent recommendation.
