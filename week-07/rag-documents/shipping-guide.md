# Soundway shipping and replacement guide

Fictional internal reference. Version 1.0, effective 1 September 2026.

## SHIP-01 Delivery estimates

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. All cut-off times use Korea Standard Time, KST.

| Service | Estimated time after dispatch | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 2 to 3 business days | Mainland addresses |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | Mainland addresses |
| Island delivery | 3 to 5 business days | Island addresses, including Jeju |

These times are estimates, not delivery guarantees. Express service is unavailable for island addresses.

## SHIP-02 Wrong-item replacements

Soundway provides free express shipping for a verified wrong-item replacement to a mainland address. Island replacements use island delivery at no charge.

An in-stock replacement is dispatched on the same business day when support completes verification and confirms the address before 14:00 KST. Verification completed at or after 14:00 moves dispatch to the next business day. Do not confirm dispatch before checking stock.

## SHIP-03 Customer deadlines

Ask for the delivery location when it is missing. State the applicable estimate only after the location, stock, and dispatch conditions are known. Do not promise delivery by the customer's requested date.
