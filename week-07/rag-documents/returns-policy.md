# Soundway returns and refunds policy

Fictional internal policy. Version 1.0, effective 1 September 2026.

## RET-01 Change of mind

A customer can request a change-of-mind return within 30 calendar days after delivery. The product must be unopened and unused. The customer pays return shipping. A refund follows inspection of the returned product.

This condition applies to a correct, working product. Wrong-item deliveries and reported defects follow the separate sections below.

## RET-02 Wrong item delivered

A customer must report a wrong-item delivery within 14 calendar days after delivery. Opening the packaging to identify the item does not disqualify the claim.

After support verifies the mismatch, the customer can choose a replacement of the ordered model or a refund. Soundway pays return shipping. The customer does not need to return the wrong item before an approved replacement is dispatched. Support provides a prepaid return label separately.

Replacement availability and delivery estimates follow the shipping guide. Support must not promise an arrival date from this policy alone.

## RET-03 Reported defects

Support reviews reported defects within the product's warranty period. The product catalog supplies that period. An opened package does not disqualify a defect report.

Support records the symptoms and purchase details, then requests a warranty review. The review determines the remedy. A report within the warranty period does not automatically qualify for a refund or replacement.
