## 2. Findings

### autumn-sale: fewer clicks and fewer purchases on the same spend (ROAS 3.50 → 2.24)

- Spend stayed at $780 and impressions barely moved (52,000 → 51,000, −1.9%).
- Clicks fell from 1,300 to 1,020 (−21.5%), so CTR dropped from 2.50% to 2.00%.
- Purchases fell faster, from 39 to 25 (−35.9%), so conversion rate dropped from 3.00% to 2.45%.
- Revenue fell from $2,730 to $1,750 (−$980). Revenue per purchase was $70 in both weeks, so
  the whole drop comes from fewer purchases, not smaller orders.
- Cost per purchase rose from $20.00 to $31.20 (+56.0%).

This breaks the campaign's earlier pattern: from 2026-09-07 to 2026-10-12 its CTR held at
about 2.5% and conversion rate at about 3.0%.

Questions to investigate:

- Did the ad creative, offer or discount change in the week of 19 October?
- Is the autumn sale winding down or has it ended, so the ads promise something the site no longer offers?
- Did targeting or placements change, bringing in a less interested audience at the same impression volume?
- Did the landing page, stock levels or checkout change for sale items that week?
- Did purchase tracking or attribution change for this campaign?

The other three campaigns are flat (all changes under 4%) and are left out of the findings.
